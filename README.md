## Notation Shorthand

- `HH`: hi-hat
- `S`: snare
- `K`: kick
- Eighth-note count grid: `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`
- Sixteenth-note count grid: `1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a`

Example:

```text
Count: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
HH:    x x x x x x x x
S:         o       o
K:    o       o
```
