## Pattern

Near-ending "boots and cats" groove.

- Kick: beat 1, "boots"
- Ride: every `&`, "tss"
- Snare: beats 2 and 4, "cats"

![Uptown Funk ride groove notation](../../notation/lazslo-lessons/uptown-funk-ride-groove-2026-07-02.svg)

## Transcription

The phrase is practiced as eighth notes: `boots tss cats tss boots tss cats tss`.
